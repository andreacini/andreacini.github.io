  • Torch Spatiotemporal (tsl)

    Co-lead

    A PyTorch library built to accelerate research on deep learning for spatiotemporal data processing, with a focus on graph neural networks. Built on PyTorch, PyG and PyTorch Lightning, it covers the whole pipeline from data preprocessing to model prototyping, with ready-to-use datasets, baselines and modular spatiotemporal layers. I co-lead its development with Ivan Marisca.
