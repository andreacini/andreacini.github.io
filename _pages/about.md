### About

Hi! I'm a SNSF postdoc fellow at the [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/), working in the [IMOS](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/imos/people/) lab led by Prof. [Olga Fink](https://people.epfl.ch/olga.fink?lang=en). My research focuses on machine learning methods for time series forecasting and graph processing and is funded by the [Swiss National Science Foundation](https://www.snf.ch/en).

I am also affiliated with [IDSIA](https://www.idsia.ch/), at [USI](https://www.usi.ch/it), and the [Northernmost Graph Machine Learning Group](https://ngmlgroup.github.io/), at the [UiT the Arctic University of Norway](https://en.uit.no/startsida). I am a proud member of the [ELLIS Society](https://ellis.eu/).

You can have a look at my [CV](../assets/pdf/cv_mar2026.pdf) and find an updated list of my publications on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=bQI2UIUAAAAJ&hl=en).

### Short bio

Before EPFL, I was an SNSF postdoc at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/) with Prof. [Michael Bronstein](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/michael.bronstein/). I obtained my PhD from [USI](https://www.usi.ch/en) under the supervision of Prof. [Cesare Alippi](https://alippi.faculty.polimi.it), with a thesis on graph deep learning methods for time series forecasting. I have been a visiting researcher at [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/) with Prof. [Danilo Mandic](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/people/d.mandic) and previously got my MSc and BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at [Politecnico di Milano](https://www.polimi.it/en/). 

My research has been published in top-tier machine learning venues, including JMLR, NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR, where I also frequently serve as a reviewer. My work on graph-based forecasting has received the [Informatics Europe Best Dissertation Award 2025](https://www.informatics-europe.org/news/968-usi-graduate-wins-record-breaking-ie-best-dissertation-award-2025.html).  I am one of the creators of [Torch Spatiotemporal](https://torch-spatiotemporal.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).
